## AREA 1 — Team Operations & Org Structure

**Required staff (UCI rules).** A WorldTeam must employ a minimum of four UCI-licensed sports directors (one per eight riders), a full-time performance director or coach, a head doctor, and trainers (≥1 per 8–10 riders). From 2026, mandatory elements include individualized rider training plans and a **digital logbook for performance monitoring** — a regulatory push toward data infrastructure. https://www.uci.org/sport-directors-course/48Ef1hsYbhIcz0GZG7DCnW , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCI_WorldTeam

**Org shape.** Strategic apex = General Manager / Team Principal. Below sit Sports Directors (race tactics from the car), plus parallel units: mechanics, physios, chefs, logistics, medical. A WorldTour team commonly runs 60+ staff, more with a development squad under the same structure. https://www.siroko.com/blog/c/guide-to-understanding-professional-road-cycling-teams/ , https://rydecruz.com/blogs/pedal-press/uci-worldtour-explained-the-hidden-structure-behind-pro-cyclings-elite-races

**Dedicated data staff.** Yes at the top end — teams employ performance engineers, data scientists, and analysts, though often blended with sports-science/coaching. British Cycling (the GB federation) has openly recruited a "Senior Data Scientist (Data & Insights Lead)" (~£42k–£84k), signalling the role is now a defined function, not ad hoc. https://talents.studysmarter.co.uk/companies/british-cycling/senior-data-scientist-data-insights-lead-6409926/

**Budgets (2026).** Total men's WorldTour spend ≈ €663M; average ≈ €33M, median ≈ €28M. Top bracket: UAE Team Emirates-XRG and Visma | Lease a Bike ≈ €50M; Lidl-Trek, Red Bull-Bora, INEOS ≈ €45M. Second tier (EF, Movistar, Soudal-QuickStep) ≈ €30M. Relegation-threatened teams < €25M; smallest (Arkéa) ≈ €17M. https://www.domestiquecycling.com/en/news/bigger-budgets-bigger-gaps-worldtour-spending-hits-eur663-million/ , https://cyclinguptodate.com/cycling/rising-budgets-and-salaries-widen-gap-in-worldtour-as-costs-surge-ahead-of-2026

**Business model.** ~87% of WorldTour team revenue comes directly from sponsors (naming rights + brand visibility). No franchise value, no gate/broadcast revenue to teams — a fragile, renewal-dependent model that raises the stakes on *proving* sponsor ROI. https://www.theleadout.cc/cycling-trends/professional-cycling-business-model/ , https://veloracycling.com/features/why-brands-fund-pro-cycling-fragile-business-model

**Where roles get posted.** Rarely on public job boards. Federations (British Cycling) post openly; WorldTeams hire mostly through personal networks, ex-rider pipelines, and existing staff referrals (see Bahrain's staff list — nearly all ex-pros). https://www.indeed.com/q-Cycling-Analytics-jobs.html , https://bahraincyclingteam.com/team/ — *Implication: cold outreach direct to decision-makers is a viable, expected channel.*

## AREA 2 — Bahrain Victorious (bahraincyclingteam.com)

**Ownership & funding.** Title sponsor and financier is the **Government of Bahrain**; the team exists to promote the Kingdom worldwide (soft-power / nation-branding vehicle). Concept initiated Aug 2016 by **Sheikh Nasser bin Hamad Al Khalifa** (royal patron). Founded 2017 (Bahrain–Merida → Bahrain–McLaren 2020 → Bahrain Victorious 2021). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Bahrain_Victorious

**Backing entities/sponsors** (partners page): Bahrain EDB, BAPCO, Mumtalakat (the sovereign wealth fund), Al-Salam Bank, BBK, Alba (aluminium smelter), RP Group. Tech/data partners: **TrainingPeaks** (training analytics platform) and **Beyon** (Bahrain's national telecom/tech group). https://bahraincyclingteam.com/partners/

## AREA 3 — Data/Tech Culture Across Top Teams (competitive positioning)

**Visma | Lease a Bike — the data leaders.** Most publicized data operation. Runs a **"Control Room"** (a tech van aggregating real-time raw data streams into one dashboard during stages), uses **Smartbase** for athlete data management/visualisation, and an **ML nutrition-prediction model** (FoodCoach app, ~82–90% calorie-need accuracy vs. 52% manual). Visma's own software also runs the team's back office (accounting, HR, contracts). This is the benchmark. https://www.visma.com/insights/content/inside-the-team-visma-lease-a-bike-control-room , https://www.visma.com/insights/content/how-ai-optimises-performance-for-team-visma-lease-a-bike , https://suresync.nl/en/blog/how-machine-learning-helps-team-visma-lease-a-bike-fuel-up

**INEOS Grenadiers — marginal-gains legacy, reinvesting.** Perf Director Scott Drawer. Publicly leaning into data science/AI: an **AWS case study** (generative AI + predictive models), a **Swansea University** partnership for AI talent-ID, a 2025 **TeamWork** data-management partnership, and a €100M/5-yr **Netcompany** (Danish AI firm) title deal rebranding to "Netcompany Ineos" — explicitly to "unlock tech and human performance." `[Netcompany rebrand: reported April 2026]`. https://aws.amazon.com/solutions/case-studies/ineos-grenadiers-generative-ai/ , https://www.cyclingweekly.com/racing/data-science-and-ai-are-the-next-frontier-for-the-sport-ineos-grenadiers-partner-with-swansea-university-to-help-find-the-next-generational-talent , https://dnlbenson.substack.com/p/from-marginal-gains-to-machine-learning

**UAE Team Emirates-XRG — performance science powerhouse.** Head of Performance **Jeroen Swart** (physiologist). Deep power/physiology-driven training (torque work, 40/20s, kJ-loss modelling of Pogačar's late-race durability). Strong on sports-science method; less publicly framed around cloud/AI platforms than Visma. https://cyclinguptodate.com/cycling/tadej-normally-loses-less-than-1-uae-performance-chief-reveals-frightening-reason-pogacar-can-still-attack-when-rivals-fade , https://www.cyclingweekly.com/fitness/training/how-tadej-pogacar-and-uae-team-emirates-train-to-be-the-1-team-in-cycling

**Race-level / ecosystem tech.** The **Tour de France** data layer is run by **NTT (ex-Dimension Data)** for organiser A.S.O. since 2015 — sensors on every bike, **150M+ data points/stage**, edge/AI race predictors, and fan-engagement analytics (A.S.O. social following grew 2.7M→7M, 2014–2019). This is organiser-side, not team-side — a template for the fan/broadcast data opportunity. https://services.global.ntt/en-us/tourdefrance , https://www.cyclingnews.com/news/artificial-intelligence-and-the-tour-de-france-ntts-race-predictors/ , https://www.computerweekly.com/news/252488985/NTT-taps-data-analytics-and-cloud-for-Tour-de-France

**Lidl-Trek / EF Education / Soudal Quick-Step.** ~€30–45M budgets; competitive but no comparably-publicised proprietary data platform surfaced in this pass. `[UNCERTAIN — not deeply searched; treat as "no strong public data brand" rather than "no data work"]`. https://cyclinguptodate.com/cycling/rising-budgets-and-salaries-widen-gap-in-worldtour-as-costs-surge-ahead-of-2026

**Where Bahrain sits.** **Behind the leaders on public data maturity.** Visma (platform + control room + ML), INEOS (AWS/Netcompany/academic AI), UAE (physiology science) all have visible programs. Bahrain has no published analytics identity — a clear whitespace and, combined with its results-vs-talent gap, an opening for a data/AI value proposition.

## AREA 4 — Business/Ops Angle (beyond rider watts)

**Sponsorship ROI measurement.** Sponsors increasingly demand quantified ROI on multi-year deals; the shift is from crude logo-impression counts to integrated measurement across social, web, and in-venue data. Brands integrating cross-channel data report ~47% higher ROI than siloed measurement. For a government funder (Bahrain), the "ROI" is nation-brand exposure and B2B relationships — inherently a **measurement/analytics problem**. https://sportfive.com/beyond-the-match/insights/understanding-pro-cyclings-unique-sponsorship-landscape , https://www.wehave.io/insights/ultimate-guide-to-sponsorship-roi-analytics

**Media/brand value & fan engagement.** Much tangible ROI is relational (hospitality on climbs, client days, year-round content), not just TV logo-seconds — all quantifiable with the right attribution model. Fan-engagement analytics (social value, audience growth) proven at organiser scale by NTT/A.S.O. https://sportfive.com/beyond-the-match/insights/sponsorships-in-cycling-how-the-biggest-brands-succeed , https://services.global.ntt/en-us/newsroom/ntt-and-aso-revolutionize-fan-experience-tour-de-france

**Logistics / calendar / travel.** WorldTeams run 60+ staff across a global calendar — race-selection, travel, and freight optimisation are classic operations-research/analytics problems, largely un-automated at most teams. `[INFERENCE from org scale; no team-specific case study found]`. https://www.siroko.com/blog/c/guide-to-understanding-professional-road-cycling-teams/

**Scouting/transfer analytics.** Emerging as "the next frontier" — INEOS+Swansea are formalising AI talent-ID; most teams still scout on eye + basic results. A data-driven recruitment/transfer model is a credible, high-leverage business function. https://www.cyclingweekly.com/racing/data-science-and-ai-are-the-next-frontier-for-the-sport-ineos-grenadiers-partner-with-swansea-university-to-help-find-the-next-generational-talent

**Women's & development teams.** Women's team budgets can be ~1/10th of men's but growing far faster (women's sports revenue grew 4.5x faster than men's, 2022–2024) — under-analysed, high-growth, ripe for a data-led commercial case. Bahrain runs a men's WorldTeam + U23 development squad (Omrzel/Borgo pipeline); no women's WorldTeam surfaced. https://www.paritynow.com/resources/research/wrapped-top-womens-sports-research-of-2025 , https://www.procyclingstats.com/team/bahrain-victorious-development-team-2026/overview/start

**Where a business-data/AI leader (not a sports scientist) fits.** Sponsorship-ROI/nation-brand measurement, fan & social analytics, commercial/partnership intelligence, logistics optimisation, scouting-analytics infrastructure, and the "data platform + cloud" plumbing that lets sports scientists work faster — i.e., the layer *around* the physiologists, which Bahrain conspicuously lacks in public.
